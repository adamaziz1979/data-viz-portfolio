Rules these samples follow (draft)

  • The data's job picks the form. A single headline is a tile, not a chart.
  • One measure, one colour. Colour only when it means something: an entity, a state or a direction.
  • Colour follows the entity. Claude Code is always blue, ChatGPT always grey, on every chart.
  • Running totals: label each period with what it added, so the labels add up to the total.
  • Bars 24px thick at most, 4px rounded end, square at the baseline. Lines 2px. Dots 9px with a ring.
  • Gridlines are hairline and solid. Axes are muted. Never two y-axes on one chart.
  • Legend below the chart for two or more series. Direct labels on line ends, never on every point.
  • Text stays in text colours, never in the series colour.
  • Status is always icon, word and colour together: ✓ On track, ! Behind.
  • Every chart has hover detail and a "See data" table.
  • Side by side charts share a top edge and height. Captions are small and muted.
  • Charts animate on first view. Click the chart to replay. No play buttons. Nothing moves with reduced motion on.
  • Dark by default. Light mode is its own checked palette, not an automatic flip.
  • No sideways scrolling from 375px to 1600px. Every control at least 44px tall.
  • Every palette passes the colourblind check before it is used.
  • Progress rings only for one percentage against a whole or a target, with the number in the middle in text colour.
  • Small multiples share one scale, so the minis can be compared at a glance.
  • Bubbles are sized by area, in one colour, with only the few that matter labelled.
  • Sunburst only for a two-level part of a whole. To compare sizes, use bars.
  • Experimental charts are for covers and summaries. Each says which rule it bends and keeps a "See data" table.
  • Sankey bands take the colour of the one stage that matters most (here the category), and their width is the count.
  • A soft fill (10% of the line colour) goes under single-series lines only, never under two or more.
